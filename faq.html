Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

What is Orion Private Wealth Consulting?

Orion is a private intermediary for clients seeking discreet, expert access to the world's finest assets — real estate, automobiles, and timepieces. We introduce, negotiate, and stay aligned with our clients throughout, across Dubai, the Swiss Alps, and the French Riviera.

How does your success-fee model work?

Orion operates on a pure success-fee basis. We earn a fee only when a transaction is completed on our client's behalf — nothing before, nothing regardless. Our interests are perfectly aligned with yours.

Which markets and asset categories do you cover?

We focus on three categories — luxury real estate, automobiles, and timepieces — across select geographies including Dubai and the wider UAE, the Engadin region of Switzerland, and the Côte d'Azur.

Is there a minimum transaction value to work with Orion?

Orion works by mandate, not by volume. Each engagement is considered individually, in a private conversation, to determine fit on both sides.

How confidential is the process?

Discretion is foundational to how we operate. We do not disclose client identities, do not publicize transaction details, and structure every mandate around privacy from first contact to closing.

Do you work directly with developers and dealers, or only as an intermediary?

Orion acts as an intermediary — we introduce clients to opportunities and negotiate on their behalf. Through our strategic partnership with Damac Properties, one of Dubai's foremost developers, Orion clients benefit from privileged access to landmark projects across the UAE.

What is your relationship with Damac Properties?

Orion maintains a strategic partnership with Damac Properties, giving our clients access and context that would otherwise require a direct developer relationship.

How do I begin a conversation with Orion?

Reach out directly through our contact page. Every engagement begins with a private conversation — no forms, no pressure.
